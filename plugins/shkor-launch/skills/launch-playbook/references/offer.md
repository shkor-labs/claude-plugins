# Step 1. Frame the offer

Goal: one sentence a stranger understands in 5 seconds, plus one buyer and one price.

Ask the user, and write the answers into `LAUNCH.md` at the repo root (create it if missing):

1. **Who pays?** One concrete person, not "everyone". Role + situation:
   "a solo physio who loses 3 hours a week on scheduling".
2. **What pain, in their words?** What they'd type into Google or complain about in a chat.
3. **Promise.** `<Outcome> for <buyer> without <main pain>.` No feature lists.
4. **Price.** One number in USD and one interval (`month`, `year` or `one_time`).
   Anchor it on 3 alternatives the buyer already uses or pays for (a tool, a freelancer,
   doing it by hand). Write them down with their prices. If you can't find 3, the buyer
   is probably vague. Go back to question 1.
5. **Kill criterion.** What result after the first test (step 7) means "stop or change
   the offer". Example: "< 1% of live visitors click Buy after $10 of ads".

Why one price: with tiers and discounts you can't tell whether the offer or the
pricing failed. Add pricing tiers after the first sale.

Template for `LAUNCH.md`:

```markdown
# Launch
- Buyer:
- Pain (their words):
- Promise:
- Price: $   / interval
- Alternatives: 1) … $…  2) … $…  3) … $…
- Kill criterion:
- URL:
- Payment link:
- Status: framing | shipped | charging | legal | traffic | reading
```
